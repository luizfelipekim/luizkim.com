Team
5 direct reports, 8 matrixed ambassadors

Situation

40+ brands, built in silos

Entain's brands spanned three product areas and six regions: the US, Canada, LATAM, Iberia, the UK and Germany. Teams worked in silos and duplicated work. Rollouts were slow, and brands drifted apart. An older system existed, but a tech-stack change meant we had to start again.

Task

One system every region would adopt

My job was to build the new system on the new tech stack and get teams in all six regions to adopt it.

Action

Three decisions to get adoption

Run the design system as a product

Nobody owned the system beyond shipping components. I set up a product process: investigate, design, build, integrate.

Cost: every product team worked differently, so each new PM needed onboarding. The process kept changing as we learned.

Decentralise ownership

I gave eight ambassadors their part of the system, some by product and some by market. DesignOps kept the structure: tokens, governance, the toolchain and the reports.

Cost: less central control, more maintenance, fewer components built for one product.

Make the system self-serve

Teams found answers in Figma, Storybook, zeroheight, Jira and a video library instead of raising tickets. DesignOps spent its time on delivery.

Emerging markets pushed back

They needed speed and flexibility, and some of their needs differed from other markets. Their ambassadors brought those needs into the system.

Result

56% faster design, 43% faster development

The system won a zeroheight award for collaboration in 2025.
