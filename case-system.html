Situation

40+ brands, built in silos

Entain's 40+ brands spanned three product areas: Gaming, Sports and Core. BetMGM, Entain's joint venture with MGM Resorts, and its sister brands covered the US and the rest of the world. Teams worked in silos and duplicated work. Rollouts were slow, and brands drifted apart. A tech-stack change meant the old system had to start again.

Task

Every team on Dice

Entain and BetMGM built Dice as one shared design system. The goal was 100% adoption across Gaming, Sports and Core. My job was to get 60+ designers there. I ran the system as a product: investigate, design, build, integrate.

Action

Three decisions to get adoption

Recruit an ambassador in each region

I went after designers in Canada, Iberia and LATAM and gave each one their part of the system, by product or by market. They worked alongside BetMGM's team. DesignOps kept the tokens, governance, toolchain and reports.

Cost: less central control, more maintenance, fewer components built for one product.

Bring in an accessibility expert

I brought an accessibility expert into the system team. Accessibility went into the core components, so no brand had to add it on its own.

Record the system for people who missed the call

Our monthly calls covered 60+ designers, but not everyone could join. I drove an education hub: an edited video for each foundational component, documented in zeroheight. I also ran sessions for the whole organisation on the token structure and semantic token usage.

Cost: every component video had to be recorded and edited, on top of delivery.

Emerging markets pushed back

They needed speed and flexibility, and some of their needs differed from other markets. Their ambassadors brought those needs into the system, so Dice did not slow them down.

Result

Every market designed with Dice, except two UK brands

Those two brands were moving to the same tech stack when I left. Dice put 40+ brands on one set of tokens. My team and I also won Best Collaboration at the zeroheight Design System Awards 2025, together with BetMGM.
