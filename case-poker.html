The problem

No testing, no personas, no trust

No user testing. No real personas.
Decisions ran on assumptions.
Product didn't trust UX.
The goal: win casual players, without knowing who they were.

What I led

1 · Brought product into the room

Product doubted research. So I sat them in live sessions with real players.

2 · Made research routine

Stakeholder interviews in week one. A standing player panel. Tests before the sprint, not after.

3 · Changed an industry default

Poker avatars meant hypersexualised women and childlike cartoons. Casual players wanted better.

Product pushed back. Players won. The realistic set shipped.

Then I wrote an internal piece on inequality in gaming, for teams beyond Poker.

Traded off: the look the industry knew.
